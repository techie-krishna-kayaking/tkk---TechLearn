## 🏆 What Interviewers Are Looking For

### **Technical (60% of decision)**
- ✅ Can you think at scale? (1B events/day, 100TB data)
- ✅ Do you know trade-offs? (Batch vs stream, cost vs latency)
- ✅ Production experience? (Can you debug + monitor?)
- ✅ Problem-solving? (When stuck, show thinking process)

### **Behavioral (40% of decision)**
- ✅ Ownership mentality (not just executing, but thinking)
- ✅ Collaboration (worked with analysts, engineers, product)
- ✅ Humility (learned from mistakes, ask for help)
- ✅ Communication (explain technical to non-technical)
- ✅ Growth mindset (learning new tools, technologies)

## 🎯 Interview Checklist

### **Before Interview**
- ☐ Reviewed handbook relevant to this company
- ☐ Did 3 mock interviews
- ☐ Understand company tech stack
- ☐ Have 5 stories ready (technical + behavioral)
- ☐ Know current market rates (Levels.fyi)

### **During Interview**
- ☐ Ask clarifying questions (don't assume)
- ☐ Think out loud (interviewer wants to see your process)
- ☐ Trade-offs > perfect solution (shows maturity)
- ☐ Metrics matter (5x improvement, $50K savings, not just "faster")
- ☐ Ask about their challenges (shows interest)

### **After Interview**
- ☐ Send thank you note (within 24 hours)
- ☐ Reflect on what went well / what to improve
- ☐ Update study plan if weak areas found
