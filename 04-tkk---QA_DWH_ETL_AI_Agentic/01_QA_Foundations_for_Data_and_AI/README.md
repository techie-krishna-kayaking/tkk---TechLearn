# 01 — QA Foundations for Data and AI

## QA focus

Define SUT boundaries, test levels, risk-based strategy, traceability and release governance for data and AI products.

## What to understand (technology minimum)

Batch pipelines move records through stages; AI systems transform inputs into probabilistic outputs. Know the inputs, transformations, outputs, dependencies, identities and service-level objectives.

## Test design prompts

- Which business rules are safety-, financial-, regulatory- or customer-critical?
- Where can records be missing, duplicated, stale, reordered or silently altered?
- What is the oracle for an AI output when there is no single exact answer?

## Practice deliverables

Create a risk register, test pyramid, traceability matrix and release criteria for a customer-analytics pipeline with an AI assistant on top. Include negative, boundary, recovery and production-monitoring coverage.

## Interview probe

How do you persuade a delivery team to block a release when all functional tests pass but reconciliation and observability evidence are incomplete?
