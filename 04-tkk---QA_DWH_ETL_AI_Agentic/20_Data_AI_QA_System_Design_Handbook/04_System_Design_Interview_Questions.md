# Data & AI QA System Design — Interview Q&A

**How do you start QA system design?** Clarify SUT/outcomes/scale/SLOs/data sensitivity, draw flows, prioritize risks, then propose test layers, data/oracles, evidence/gates and production controls.

**What is the QA platform’s job?** Execute/coordinate validations and evaluations, retain evidence, surface metrics/defects and make PASS/FAIL/BLOCK decisions reproducible.

**How do you scale testing?** Risk tiers, reusable validators/contracts, representative synthetic data, parallelizable suites, evidence storage and environment isolation.

**What trade-off would you state?** Exhaustive testing is impossible; prioritize critical controls and use monitoring/sampling for lower-risk uncertainty, with residual risk owned explicitly.

**How does AI change design?** Add versioned gold sets, rubric/human calibration, safety/privacy tests, trace capture, tool/authority checks and probabilistic regression thresholds.
