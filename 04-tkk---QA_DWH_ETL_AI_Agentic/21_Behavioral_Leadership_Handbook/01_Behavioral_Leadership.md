# 21 — Behavioral & Quality Leadership Handbook

## STAR-L framework

**Situation**: business/system context. **Task**: quality risk and your mandate. **Action**: what you did, including influence and evidence. **Result**: measured outcome. **Learning**: process/control improvement.

## Stories to prepare

- Blocked or changed a release based on evidence.
- Found a high-impact defect before production.
- Led a production data/AI incident.
- Resolved disagreement with engineering/product through risk framing.
- Improved flaky/slow automation and confidence.
- Established quality gate, monitoring or ownership model.
- Handled ambiguous AI-quality acceptance criteria.

Quantify impact honestly: prevented incorrect reporting, reduced MTTD/MTTR, improved coverage of critical rules, reduced escaped defects or accelerated safe release feedback.
