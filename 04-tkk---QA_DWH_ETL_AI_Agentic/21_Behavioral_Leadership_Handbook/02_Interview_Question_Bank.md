# Leadership Question Bank

- Tell me about a time you challenged a release decision.
- How do you balance delivery speed and quality risk?
- Describe an incident where your first hypothesis was wrong.
- How do you get teams to own data quality?
- How do you communicate probabilistic AI quality to non-technical stakeholders?
- What metrics show a quality program is working?
- How do you handle a critical flaky test?

Answer each with STAR-L, a decision trade-off, stakeholder communication and a preventive control.
