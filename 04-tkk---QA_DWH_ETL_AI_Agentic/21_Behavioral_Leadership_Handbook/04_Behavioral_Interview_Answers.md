# Behavioral & Leadership — Interview Answers

**How do you influence when QA lacks release authority?** Translate test evidence into customer/business/regulatory risk, offer options/controls and ensure the accountable owner records the final decision.

**Tell me about blocking a release.** Use STAR-L: critical risk, evidence/oracle, stakeholders, containment, decision, measurable outcome and preventive automation/monitor added.

**How do you balance speed and quality?** Risk-tier coverage: automate fast critical gates, use explicit waiver/compensating controls for uncertainty and avoid late surprise by shifting contracts/tests left.

**How do you lead an incident?** Establish impact/command/evidence stream, contain, communicate factually, validate recovery and drive blameless corrective/preventive action.

**What defines QA leadership?** Making ownership, risk, quality signal and decision transparent across teams—not being the last tester before deployment.
