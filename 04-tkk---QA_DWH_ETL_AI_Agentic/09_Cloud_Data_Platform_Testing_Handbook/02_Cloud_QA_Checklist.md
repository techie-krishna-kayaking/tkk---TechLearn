# Cloud QA Checklist

- [ ] Storage/object/table access is least privilege and tenant-scoped.
- [ ] Encryption at rest/in transit and key access meet requirements.
- [ ] Secret rotation/expiry produces safe, observable behavior.
- [ ] Backup/restore and retention policy are tested with evidence.
- [ ] Scaling/failover maintains correctness and SLA.
- [ ] Audit logs include actor, resource, action, result and trace ID.
