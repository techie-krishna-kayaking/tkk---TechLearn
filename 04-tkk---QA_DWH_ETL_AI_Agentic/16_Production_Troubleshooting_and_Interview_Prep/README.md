# 16 — Production Troubleshooting and Interview Preparation

## QA focus

Lead evidence-driven incident investigation, communicate risk clearly, drive corrective/preventive actions and articulate quality architecture in senior interviews.

## Investigation sequence

Confirm scope and customer impact; preserve evidence; compare to baseline; check freshness/volume/schema/reconciliation; trace the failing record or request; isolate the change; mitigate; validate recovery; document root cause and prevention.

## Senior interview structure

State assumptions and SUT boundary, draw the quality risks, prioritize by impact/likelihood, propose test layers and data, define gates/metrics, cover failure/recovery/security, then explain production observability and leadership actions.

## Practice

Rehearse a 10-minute answer to: “Design the test strategy for a regulated financial RAG agent that can query warehouse data but must never perform an unauthorized action.”
