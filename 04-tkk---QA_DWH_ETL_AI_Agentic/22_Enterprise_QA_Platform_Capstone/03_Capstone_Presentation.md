# Capstone Presentation — 15 Minutes

| Time | Content |
|---:|---|
| 2 min | SUT, outcomes, scope and critical risks |
| 3 min | Test strategy, risk prioritization and data/oracle model |
| 3 min | Data/API/AI evaluation architecture and evidence |
| 3 min | Performance/security/recovery and representative defects |
| 2 min | CI/CD gates, monitoring, rollback and release decision |
| 2 min | Trade-offs, ownership and leadership impact |

End with the evidence required to move from BLOCK to PASS.
