# 22 — Enterprise Data & AI QA Platform Capstone

> Build the quality engineering platform around an enterprise Data/AI SUT. You are not building the pipeline, model, RAG application or agent; you are proving whether it is fit to release.

## Suggested SUT

A financial-services platform ingests transactions, publishes a warehouse dashboard, exposes a policy-document RAG assistant and runs an agent that may draft—but never submit—case actions.

## Required deliverables

1. SUT/context diagram and risk register.
2. Master test strategy and traceability.
3. ETL/DWH validation and reconciliation suite.
4. API contract/integration and negative-test suite.
5. ML, LLM, RAG and agent evaluation strategy/test sets.
6. Security, performance, reliability and recovery plan.
7. CI/CD gate, evidence/reporting and waiver design.
8. Three defects with evidence, root cause, corrective/preventive action.
9. Production monitoring/runbook and 15-minute leadership presentation.

## Acceptance criteria

Each critical test has an owner, test data, oracle, threshold, evidence and failure action. The final decision must be reproducible as PASS / FAIL / BLOCK.
