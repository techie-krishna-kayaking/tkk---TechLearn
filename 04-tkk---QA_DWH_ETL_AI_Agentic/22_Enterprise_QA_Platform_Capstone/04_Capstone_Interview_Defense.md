# Capstone Interview Defense

**Why is this a QA capstone rather than an AI project?** Its deliverables are strategy, validators, test data, evaluations, defects, reports, gates and monitoring—not pipeline/model/agent implementation.

**What proves readiness?** Current evidence for critical data/API/security/AI/NFR controls, no unresolved critical defects, clear gate decision and tested rollback/monitoring.

**How do you prove AI quality?** Versioned golden data, stage-level traces, deterministic/rubric criteria, human-calibrated evaluation, safety/privacy cases and regression thresholds.

**What is the most important artifact?** A reproducible PASS/FAIL/BLOCK decision backed by traceable tests, data and owned residual risk.

**What would you improve at scale?** Contract enforcement, test-data management, parallel execution, evidence observability, criticality tiers and governance for human/AI evaluation.
