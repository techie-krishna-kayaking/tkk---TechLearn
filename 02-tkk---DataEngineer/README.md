## 📖 What This Is

A **battle-tested interview prep system** for senior/staff/principal data engineer roles at top-tier product companies. Not a tutorial. Not entry-level. This is **competition-level preparation** targeting the **₹80–120 LPA** band with:

✅ **1200+ real interview questions** with model answers  
✅ **10+ production case studies** (Netflix, Uber, Amazon-scale problems)  
✅ **90+ LeetCode problems** across all difficulty levels  
✅ **Data modeling & warehousing** — Kimball, SCD, Data Vault (the most under-prepared round)  
✅ **Advanced SQL** — window functions, recursive CTEs, gaps-and-islands, query plans  
✅ **Lakehouse & table formats** — Delta vs Iceberg vs Hudi, ACID, time travel (2024–25 hot topic)  
✅ **Data quality & governance** — contracts, lineage, observability, PII/GDPR  
✅ **DataOps & IaC** — Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, CI/CD for pipelines  
✅ **Distributed systems fundamentals** — CAP, quorums, partitioning, idempotency  
✅ **Cost analysis** for every architecture (shows business thinking)  
✅ **100+ behavioral questions** with STAR method + negotiation tactics

> **Why 80–120 LPA needs more than Spark:** at 10 YOE, coding Spark/SQL is *table stakes*. The offer — and your **level** — is decided by the **senior differentiators**: modeling a warehouse on a whiteboard, reasoning about lakehouse internals, owning data trust (quality/governance/lineage), shipping pipelines like software (DataOps/IaC), and deriving designs from distributed-systems first principles. Handbooks 17–22 are exactly those differentiators.

## 🏆 What Interviewers Are Looking For

### **Technical (60% of decision)**
- ✅ Can you think at scale? (1B events/day, 100TB data)
- ✅ Do you know trade-offs? (Batch vs stream, cost vs latency)
- ✅ Can you **model data** correctly? (grain, SCD, conformed dims)
- ✅ Do you understand **lakehouse internals**? (ACID, time travel, compaction)
- ✅ Do you own **data trust**? (quality, contracts, lineage, governance)
- ✅ Do you ship like software? (Docker/K8s/Terraform/CI-CD)
- ✅ Production experience? (Can you debug + monitor?)
- ✅ Problem-solving? (When stuck, show thinking process)

### **Behavioral (40% of decision)**
- ✅ Ownership mentality (not just executing, but thinking)
- ✅ Collaboration (worked with analysts, engineers, product)
- ✅ Humility (learned from mistakes, ask for help)
- ✅ Communication (explain technical to non-technical)
- ✅ Growth mindset (learning new tools, technologies)

No prep guarantees an outcome — results depend on your delivery, the panel, headcount, timing, and how you're levelled. Treat the following as **directional confidence** *if* you complete all 24 weeks, can **perform** (not just recognize) the content, and interview at the right level:

- Google / Meta / Amazon (senior/staff DE): **competitive; expect a high modeling + system-design + DSA bar**
- Databricks / Netflix / Uber / Stripe (senior DE): **strong fit — lakehouse + distributed-systems depth maps directly to their loops**
- Tier-1 startups / unicorns (senior/staff/lead DE): **strong fit, especially with modeling + DataOps + governance**

> **Reality check on the number:** ₹80–120 LPA in India almost always means **Staff/Principal DE, Lead DE, or DE at a top-paying product company**, where total comp includes significant **equity/RSUs**. Interview for the *right level* — comp follows level (Handbook 15). Deep Spark/SQL alone rarely clears this band; the modeling, lakehouse, governance, DataOps, and distributed-systems signal (Handbooks 17–22) is what does.

## 🎯 Interview Checklist

### **Before Interview**
- ☐ Reviewed handbook relevant to this company
- ☐ Did 3 mock interviews
- ☐ Understand company tech stack
- ☐ Have 5 stories ready (technical + behavioral)
- ☐ Know current market rates (Levels.fyi)

### **During Interview**
- ☐ Ask clarifying questions (don't assume)
- ☐ Think out loud (interviewer wants to see your process)
- ☐ Trade-offs > perfect solution (shows maturity)
- ☐ Metrics matter (5x improvement, $50K savings, not just "faster")
- ☐ Ask about their challenges (shows interest)

### **After Interview**
- ☐ Send thank you note (within 24 hours)
- ☐ Reflect on what went well / what to improve
- ☐ Update study plan if weak areas found
